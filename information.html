Recognitions & Awards

The MOSER System was introduced at the Northeastern Ohio Science & Engineering Fair and has been continuously developed and iterated based upon feedback by experts and professionals across various academic competitions.

IEEE Cleveland 

Dr. Charles Alexander Award

MOSER earned it for Excellence in following the Engineering Design Process, in-determining the problem, solving it, and documenting iterations, mistakes and thought-processes across its development

Ohio Invention Convention

State Finalist

The MOSER Design System was presented at the Center of Science & Industry and received an Honorable Mention and an invitation to present at Kid Inventors Day at the Ohio Statehouse in January 2027

Ohio Young Inventors Fellowship

Fellowship Project

MOSER was presented at the Young Inventors Fellowship Showcase to several professionals and experts, enabling greater feedback and contribution to the MOSER System
