A Platform Built for Students by Students

The MOSER System is a free and open-source educational robotics design system for hobby-grade components

Enabling educators to implement STEM Education Programs based in robotics at a far lower entry and maintenance cost.

3 Guiding Principles

Promote 3D Printing

The system should be designed to promote the FDM manufacturing of structural parts within the school, limiting cost and enabling greater creativity within a schools implementation of robotics education

Utilize Readily Available Components

Emphasize the usage of readily available electronics and components rather than developing proprietary components and making a single point of failure in the implementation and maintenance of the system

Free and Open Source

Should be open-source, allowing students and educators to develop new additions to the design system, allowing the implementation of a wider range of sensors, control systems, and batteries under a uniform design system.

The MOSER Structural System

Structure

MOSER Pattern

The MOSER Pattern is metric-based and designed to be 3D Printed and versatile for students to use.

Motion

6mm D-Shaft

The 6mm D-Shaft was chosen as it is a readily available component and is already used in Educational Robotics.

Mount

M4 Screws

These were chosen for versatility, being readily available and is already used in Educational Robotics.
